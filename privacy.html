Privacy notice

Willis Vault Backup · Effective September 21, 2026

Purpose and access

This personal-use utility uses Google Drive authorization solely to create, read and manage its backup files, inspect storage availability, and perform owner-requested restores. It requests access to files created by the app. It is not an advertising, analytics or AI-training service.

Storage and sharing

Restic encrypts backup contents before Rclone uploads them to the owner's Google Drive. Credentials and recovery keys are kept in private owner-controlled storage, not on this website or in its public repository. Backup data is not sold or shared with advertisers. Google processes stored backups under its own service terms. People the owner explicitly authorizes may administer the backup or recovery credentials.

Retention and control

The owner controls backup retention. No automatic pruning is enabled by this setup. Access can be revoked through the Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revoking access does not itself delete previously stored backups; the owner can manage those through Google Drive.

This website

These static information pages do not request a login, collect backup files or add analytics. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process ordinary web-request information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact

This utility is operated for personal use. The support contact is the address displayed on its Google authorization screen.

Use of Google API information follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
